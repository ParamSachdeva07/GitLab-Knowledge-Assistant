## Content library

The following sections provide an overview of all content assets and links to find them. You can search for relevant content on this page and contact the team in the linked content epics or in the [#developer-advocacy](https://app.slack.com/client/T02592416/CMELFQS4B) Slack channel.

For demos, choose the format that fits your audience and environment:

- [Product tours](#product-tours): Short, guided walkthroughs that introduce a feature and its value.
- [Click-through demos](#click-through-demos): Product simulations for rehearsing or presenting a workflow without a live environment, including offline use.
- [Projects](#projects): Demo environments for hands-on exploration, workshops, and live demonstrations. Review each environment's setup instructions before use.

## Content creation guidelines

### Video production guidelines

#### Equipment recommendations

**Microphone**

- Recommended: [Logitech Blue Yeti](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/shop/p/yeti-premium-usb-microphone)
- Use the same microphone setup for all recordings to ensure consistent audio quality across videos
- Test your microphone levels before recording to ensure smooth, clear audio
- Position the microphone consistently (6-12 inches from your mouth) to maintain audio quality

**Camera**

- Recommended: [Logitech MX Brio Ultra HD 4k](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-brio-4k-webcam)
- Position at eye level for the most natural appearance

**Lighting**

- Recommended: [Elgato Key Light](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/key-light)
- Use consistent lighting for all on-camera recordings

**Background**

- Keep backgrounds clean and professional
- Avoid clutter, distracting items, or busy patterns
- Use a neutral background or a simple, organized workspace
- Ensure the background is well-lit and free of harsh shadows
- Consistency across videos creates a more professional appearance

#### Software

**Screen recording**

- Recommended tools: Quicktime, [OBS](#recording-with-obs), or [Screen Studio](#recording-with-screen-studio)

**Post-editing**

- Adobe Premiere Pro (video cuts, audio gain boost, etc.)

#### On-camera presentation tips

When recording videos with yourself on camera:

- Speak naturally and conversationally - avoid reading from scripts
- Use bullet points or talking points instead of full scripts to maintain authenticity
- Make eye contact with the camera to connect with viewers
- Be yourself - authenticity builds trust and connection with the audience
- If you make a mistake, pause and restart that section naturally rather than trying to power through
- Practice your key points beforehand, but deliver them conversationally during recording

#### Audio gain boost for professional broadcaster microphones

If using a professional broadcaster microphone (e.g., Shure SM7B) and the audio gain is too low, try these options:

**Hardware solution (recommended)**

- Audio interface + Cloudlifter greatly improves results
- The SM7B microphone needs extra gain before reaching your computer

**OBS Recording**

1. Increase audio gain in OBS during recordings
2. Locate `Source > Audio Capture Device`
3. Right-click and select `Filters`
4. Set Gain to +10 or +20dB
5. Configure filters:
   - Noise Suppression: RNNoise or Speex
   - Compressor: Ratio 3:1, Threshold -18dB, Attack 6ms, Release 60ms
   - Limiter: Threshold -6dB, Release 60ms

Tip: Ask [Claude](/handbook/tools-and-tips/ai/claude/) for best practice settings (prompt: `How to boost audio in OBS for a Shure SM7B microphone as input?`)

**Post-editing Screen Studio recordings**

1. Edit the exported recording in Adobe Premiere Pro
2. Create a new project and add the video source in the media browser
3. Drag and drop the video into the editor
4. Right-click on the audio track and select `Audio Gain`
5. Choose `Set Gain to:` and enter `25 dB`

### Product tours guidelines

#### Guidelines for creating GitLab tours with Navattic

1. **Keep it concise** - Product tours should have 5 to 12 steps, each delivering clear value. Unlike tutorials, product tours should be quick and engaging.
2. **Provide context** - Add links to blog posts and documentation in pop-up dialogs for additional resources.
3. **Apply GitLab branding** - In theme settings, select "GitLab Branding [Navattic Team]" for consistent design.
4. **Use the appropriate capture type**:
   - Web captures (preferred) create an in-product experience
   - Screenshots for non-web-based applications (e.g., IDEs)
5. **Set the correct resolution** - When capturing in Chrome, use 1440x900 (Navattic's recommended resolution). Use the built-in resize tool in the Navattic recording toolbar.
6. **Include a call to action** - The final step should feature a CTA, typically a Free Trial link.
7. **Use Beacons to highlight relevant elements** - Apply the Highlight appearance setting to emphasize key elements.
8. **Enable keyboard navigation** - Allow users to navigate the tour using arrow keys for a smoother experience.
9. **Use Presenter Mode** - Hide pop-ups when necessary for cleaner visual presentation.
10. **Set pop-ups appropriately**:
    - Modals with heavy backdrop for first and last steps
    - Tooltips for intermediate steps, enabling Backdrop setting when appropriate
11. **Use action-specific labels** - Use labels like "Commit," "Install," or "Submit" instead of "Next" to make users feel actively engaged.
12. **Review examples** - Review [existing product tours](#product-tours) to understand the preferred format.
13. **Request review** - Before releasing any public-facing product tour, request a review from the Developer Advocacy team.
