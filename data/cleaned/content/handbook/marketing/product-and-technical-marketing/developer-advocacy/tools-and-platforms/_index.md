Developer Advocacy maintains tools and platforms that help us connect with developers, support community conversations, and automate recurring work. This section brings together our cloud resources, community insights tooling, community platforms, and automation workflows.

Use the pages below to find out how we use each tool, who to contact for help, and how to manage access and day-to-day operations. The community platform guides also cover moderation, with shared guidance for enforcing the Code of Conduct.

## Tools and platforms

### Community platforms

- [Discord](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/discord/): Server participation, administration, and moderation.
- [Forum](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/forum/): Account management, moderation, and forum operations.
- [Reddit](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/reddit/): Team participation and subreddit moderation.

### Tools and operations

- [Cloud resources](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/cloud-resources/): Infrastructure access and resource management for demos and projects.
- [Common Room](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/common-room/): Community insights, reporting, and access.
- [Workato](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/workato/): Automations connecting content and community platforms.
- [Code of Conduct enforcement](/handbook/marketing/product-and-technical-marketing/developer-advocacy/tools-and-platforms/code-of-conduct-enforcement/): Handling violations and escalating community concerns.
