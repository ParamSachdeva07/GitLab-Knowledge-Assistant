## Uses

Inbox Monster is email marketing software used primarily by Lifecycle Marketing to build, test, and optimize our email marketing programs.

This page is under development.

## Access and access requests

Request access via Lumos. Currently, only Marketing team members can request access, but please contact the Marketing Ops team via Slack if you require access and do not have the tile available in Lumos. Most users will be granted a Standard User license.

Once you have been provisioned with a user license, you will access Inbox Monster via Okta.
