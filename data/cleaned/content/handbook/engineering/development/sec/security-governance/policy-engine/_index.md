## Mission

Own the evaluation core: how a policy's rules run against an input and return a decision.
That covers the OPA and Rego integration, the evaluation language choice, evaluation
performance at agentic scale, and the sandbox boundaries that keep evaluation pure and
fast.

## History

This group was created in the FY27 Sec reorg as a split of the former Security Policies
group. The `group::security policies` label history moved to
[Policy Management](/handbook/engineering/development/sec/security-governance/policy-management/),
whose charter maps more closely onto the historical scope. Policy Engine work items are
retagged after the cutover.
