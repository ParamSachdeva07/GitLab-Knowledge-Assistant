## Mission

Build and operate the GitLab Secrets Manager product, and contribute upstream.

The Application team owns the GitLab-side product surface: the Rails integration, the
APIs, the UI, and the CI/CD experience for consuming secrets.

## History

This team was created in the FY27 Sec reorg as a split of the former Pipeline Security
group and inherits its label history: `group::pipeline security` was renamed to
`group::secrets manager application`, so the label ID and all attached issues and merge
requests carried over.

Shared group pages are on the
[Secrets Manager group page](/handbook/engineering/development/sec/security-platform/secrets-manager/).
