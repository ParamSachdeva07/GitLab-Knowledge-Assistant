## Mission

Build and operate the GitLab Secrets Manager product, and contribute upstream.

The OpenBAO team owns the [OpenBAO](https://openbao.org/) backend that stores and serves
secrets, including the GitLab deployment of it and the contributions made back to the
upstream project.

This team was created in the FY27 Sec reorg as a split of the former Pipeline Security
group. The `group::pipeline security` label history stayed with the
[Application team](/handbook/engineering/development/sec/security-platform/secrets-manager/application/),
so OpenBAO metrics start at the reorg cutover.

Shared group pages are on the
