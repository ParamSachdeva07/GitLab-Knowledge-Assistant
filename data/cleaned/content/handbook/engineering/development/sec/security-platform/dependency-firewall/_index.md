This group was renamed from Dynamic Analysis in the FY27 Sec reorg, and its charter moved
from DAST and fuzzing to software supply chain security. The `group::dynamic analysis`
label was renamed to `group::dependency firewall`, so the label ID and all attached issues
and merge requests carried over and metric history is preserved.
