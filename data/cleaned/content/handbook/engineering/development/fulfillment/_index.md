## Mission

Ensure customers get the right product access and can effectively manage seats, usage, and spend through reliable entitlement, identity, visibility, and control systems across deployment types.

The Monetization and Fulfillment sections were split from a single Fulfillment section in August 2026. The [Monetization section](/handbook/engineering/development/monetization/) owns purchase, subscriptions, billing, and CustomersDot operations. Both sections follow the [shared ways of working](/handbook/engineering/development/fulfillment-monetization/).
