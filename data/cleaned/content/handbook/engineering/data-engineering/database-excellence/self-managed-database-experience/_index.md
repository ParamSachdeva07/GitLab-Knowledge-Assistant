The Self-Managed Database Experience team formed in September 2026 from the self-managed half of the [Database Health](/handbook/engineering/data-engineering/database-excellence/database-health/) team and the upgrade and migration work that had been planned for a separate Database Migrations team.

## Team

The team is composed of backend engineers with deep PostgreSQL and Rails migration experience. Regardless of role, all team members share stage-level responsibilities including database reviews, oncall rotations, and operational needs alongside the other Database Excellence teams.
