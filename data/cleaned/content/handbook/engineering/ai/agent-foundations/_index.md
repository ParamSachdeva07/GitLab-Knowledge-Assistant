## 🚀 Mission

The Agent Foundations stage builds the foundations for GitLab's agentic AI experiences. We provide the platform, catalog, and execution capabilities that help teams deliver resilient agents and flows for customers.

---

## 🧭 Groups

The stage is made up of three groups, each with its individual teams:

| Team | What they own | EM | PM | UX |
| ---- | ------------- | -- | -- | -- |
| [Agent Developer](/handbook/engineering/ai/agent-foundations/agent-developer/) | The GitLab Duo Developer foundational flow and flow registry improvements | Annie Ying | Frances Wang | N/A |
| [AI Catalog](/handbook/engineering/ai/agent-foundations/ai-catalog/) | AI Catalog, custom agents, custom flows, and external agent integrations | Jafar Soltani | Roman Kolosovskiy | Michael Moeyers |
| [Agent Execution](/handbook/engineering/ai/agent-foundations/agent-execution/) | Agentic observability, agent execution on GitLab runners, and agent tools | Sebastian Rehm | Amanda Rueda | Emily Bauman |
