## Plan:Portfolio Planning team

The Plan:Portfolio Planning team works on both the backend and frontend parts of
GitLab's [Portfolio Planning](/handbook/product/categories/#portfolio-planning-group) category in the [Plan stage](/handbook/engineering/devops/plan/).

For more details about the vision for this area of the product, see the
[Plan stage](/handbook/engineering/devops/plan/) page.
