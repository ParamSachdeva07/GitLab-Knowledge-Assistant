## What are suggestions

Suggestions is a grouping of various Zendesk Copilot features based around Copilot suggestion actions to take:

- First replies
- Tickets to merge

- Show similar tickets
- Summarize conversations
- Show intent in the header
- Show sentiment in the header
- Suggest ticket assignees (EAP)
