Once a week, we generate a summary report of our issues and notify key members of the SIG team.

## The process

### Generate a report

At the end of the week, navigate to our [issue tracker project](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/eta/css/issue-tracker). From there, open a Duo Chat and select the `CSS Summary Report` agent. Have it perform its task (you can just say `Run your task`).

It will review our issues and generate an issue containing the report.

After the issue is created, move to [Review the report](#review-the-report).

### Review the report

Review the contents of the report for any errors or bad summaries. Rectify any issues you find.

After you are done reviewing, move to [Notify others](#notify-others).

### Notify others

After you have generated and reviewed the report, add a command pinging TJ, Jeff, Kirsty, and Kasey:

```plaintext
cc @tjpais @jstone10 @KirstyAllen @ktague
```

After you have done so, close out the issue.
