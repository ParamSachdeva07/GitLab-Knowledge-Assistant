The End User Services (EUS) team is the IT Helpdesk for GitLab. We provide internal IT support for the entire organisation, covering access management, hardware procurement and support, and application support for many of our most commonly used applications.

## How to Contact Us

**Email:** it-help@gitlab.com<br>
**Slack:** Please contact IT via the Compass app in Slack (type "Compass" in the top search bar to find it).

**Courtesy reminder:** our team members have scheduled on-call shifts, so please ask via Compass or email rather than sending Slack direct messages to specific individuals (unless it's a sensitive matter). There is no need to tag any group or person — your request will be answered by the first available person.

## What We Do

We have 24x5 coverage provided by Helpdesk Support Analysts who can help you with access requests, account lockouts, authentication issues, laptop hardware, laptop software configuration problems, 1:1 training for unfamiliar technologies, and triaging problems for any [Corporate Security System](/handbook/security/corporate/systems).

**Corporate IT / EUS handles requests and enablement. [Corporate Security Identity Engineering](/handbook/security/corporate/engineering/identity) handles architecture, security controls, and identity provisioning automation.**

### We own

1. [Ad-hoc access requests](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/access-requests/) and fulfilment
1. Access builds for [Temporary Service Providers](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/temporary-users/) and [External Collaborators](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/external-collaborators/)
1. [Account password and 2FA resets](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/account-resets/)
1. [Laptop procurement, repairs, and wipes](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/laptop-management/)
1. [IT onboarding enablement](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/onboarding101/) and end user training
1. End user support for [supported applications](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/supported-apps/) and [Zoom](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/zoom/)

[CorpSec Devices](/handbook/security/corporate/engineering/devices) owns mobile
device management (MDM) and endpoint detection and response (EDR)
configuration, endpoint security baselines, browser security controls, software
update policy, and device-trust enforcement. EUS performs user support,
logistics, and delegated routine device actions within those controls.

### CorpSec Identity Engineering owns

1. Okta system management and Okta authentication
1. Automated access request flows through Lumos (Identity Governance)
1. Provisioning architecture and orchestration
1. [Onboarding](/handbook/security/corporate/services/onboarding) and [offboarding](/handbook/security/corporate/services/offboarding) automation and overall design
1. [Service Accounts](/handbook/security/corporate/services/service-accounts)

For systems not managed by Corporate IT or Corporate Security, you can locate the owner in the [tech stack](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/www-gitlab-com/-/blob/master/data/tech_stack.yml). If you're not sure where to ask, contact us and we will redirect you to the right person or place.

## Support Hours

We provide support 24x5 to support standard working hours for our team members during the work week from Sunday 22:00 UTC to Friday 23:59 UTC. We do not provide support coverage on weekends.

In the event of an emergency or urgent request, please contact IT via the Compass app in Slack (type "Compass" in the top search bar to find it) or it-help@gitlab.com.

**Courtesy Reminder:** Our team members have scheduled on-call shifts, so we kindly ask that you ask in the channel and **not send Slack direct messages to specific individuals (unless it's for a sensitive matter)**. There is no need to tag any group or person in the slack app since your request **will be responded to by the first available person**. Please contact IT via the Compass app in Slack (type "Compass" in the top search bar to find it) or it-help@gitlab.com.
