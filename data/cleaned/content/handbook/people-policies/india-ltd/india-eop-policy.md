### 1.1 Policy Statement

### 1.2 Scope

The Equal Opportunity Policy (EOP) has been implemented to comply with applicable laws and regulations which are intended to prevent discrimination against individuals with disabilities, transgender people and protected persons under HIV/AIDS Law. This EOP applies to all persons with disabilities, protected medical conditions and transgender people including team members, job applicants and candidates, interns/trainees and team members who acquire any disability during their work tenure. It covers all aspects of the employment relationship, including but not limited to, recruitment, hiring, selection for training opportunities, compensation, transfer, promotion, benefit eligibility, facilities access, corrective action and termination. The EOP focuses on eliminating artificial barriers to employment or advancement and fostering a diverse environment. Consistent with this EOP, GitLab is committed to making employment decisions based on merit, qualifications, business needs and other job-related criteria.  

GitLab will not discharge or in any other manner discriminate or retaliate against team members or applicants because they have inquired about, discussed, or disclosed their disabilities or medical information or details regarding their gender. This EOP is in line with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016, the Rights of Persons with Disability Rules 2017 (together, Disability Law), the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019 (Transgender Act) with Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Rules 2020 (together, Transgender Law) and the Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (Prevention and Control) Act 2017 and Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (Prevention And Control) Rules 2018 (together, HIV/AIDS Law).

The People Compliance team is responsible for this EOP and any necessary training on equal opportunities. The EOP does not form part of any team member's contract of employment and GitLab may amend it at any time.

### 1.3 Appointment of Liaison and Complaints Officers

As per the applicable law, GitLab has appointed its Team Member Relations and People Compliance Teams to act as Liaison and Complaints Officers. 

The Team Member Relations and People Compliance Teams will be responsible for:

- Ensuring a disability-friendly workplace - all team members are encouraged to discuss any specific requirements that they may have with the Team Member Relations Team;
- Taking initiatives and providing the requisite support needed to realize the goals of an inclusive, accessible and discrimination-free workplace and of offering reasonable accommodation;
- Overseeing the recruitment of individuals protected by this policy and the provision of facilities and amenities to them;
- Increasing awareness amongst team members of GitLab’s commitment to providing equal opportunities for all;
- Developing proactive strategies to prevent discrimination or harassment against the objectives of this EOP; and
- Addressing any grievances or concerns relating to violation of this EOP.

### 1.4 Facilities and Amenities

The following sections outline the facilities and amenities provided by GitLab in implementing and executing this EOP.

#### Digital Infrastructure

It is GitLab’s continuous endeavour to ensure that all our documents, communication and information technology systems adhere to the accessibility standards. GitLab provides a range of [accessibility and accommodation tools](/handbook/business-technology/entapps-documentation/application-accessibility/) designed to support team members and ensure an inclusive, productive work environment. If a team member requires access to an accommodation tool, they should reach out in the #enterprise-applications Slack channel for assistance. The Enterprise Team will coordinate with the Team Member Relations team for any accommodation-related requests.

#### Reasonable Accommodation

GitLab will make reasonable accommodations for eligible/qualified team members or job applicants with special needs and protected persons, to the extent required by applicable law to enable them to perform the essential functions of their positions or apply for the relevant position, as the case may be. What constitutes a reasonable accommodation may depend on many factors including, but not limited to, the nature of the individual’s special needs and medical requirements and the essential functions of the position. GitLab is not obligated to provide an accommodation that would impose an undue hardship on GitLab. 

Such accommodation would be provided: a) to ensure equal opportunity in the application and selection process, b) to enable a qualified individual to perform the essential functions of their role, and c) to enable a qualified individual to enjoy the same benefits and privileges of employment as other employees. Examples of reasonable accommodations may include (but not be limited to) a change or modification made to the work environment, role duties, or application process.

If a team member requires an accommodation to be made for them, a request for accommodation should be made by following [the reasonable accommodation request process set out in the Handbook](/handbook/people-policies/#team-members-requesting-accommodations). Candidates should follow the [candidate accessibility and accommodation request process set out in the Handbook](/handbook/hiring/talent-acquisition-framework/coordinator/#candidate-accessibility-and-accommodation-request-process). Preference may be given to persons with special needs while allotting such accommodation. 

#### Travel, Stay and Transport

While GitLab is all-remote, there may be times when team members travel for work-related functions or co-working events. GitLab is committed to the value of a safe, violence-free work environment and ensuring and exhibiting equal commitment to the safety and health of team members. Team members should adhere to any recommended safety recommendations made by the GitLab group and if support is required, team members should follow the [team member safety guidelines set out in the Handbook](/handbook/people-policies/#team-member-safety).

#### Team Member Engagement and Social Inclusion

GitLab is dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive event experience for everyone regardless of gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neurodiversity, physical appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, race, age, religion (or lack thereof), or any other protected categories with a provision of reasonable accommodation being available to team members with disabilities. Team members should adhere to the [GitLab Events Code of Conduct](/handbook/company/culture/ecoc/) set out in the Handbook and use the [GitLab Operating Principles](/handbook/company/operating-principles/#culture-of-excellence) to guide interactions. 

#### 1.5 Hiring, Recruitment and Manner of Selection 

GitLab is proud to be an equal opportunity workplace and GitLab’s policies and practices relating to recruitment and selection are based solely on merit, without regard to any legally protected characteristics. At GitLab, all positions are open for all persons. Accommodations are available on request and candidates should follow the [candidate accessibility and accommodation request process set out in the Handbook](/handbook/hiring/talent-acquisition-framework/coordinator/#candidate-accessibility-and-accommodation-request-process).

Wherever possible, vacancies will be advertised internally as well as externally. Selection criteria are kept under constant review to ensure that they are non-discriminatory and that they relate purely to the skills needed for the job and nothing else. 

#### 1.6 Other Facilities

_Leave_ - GitLab may, based on a team member's request for leave which exceeds six consecutive days, for a reason related to their disability, administer the leave as a leave of absence through the GitLab Sick Time policy, under which eligible team members will receive 100% paid leave for up to 25 working days. GitLab’s Sick Time policy is administered concurrently with any statutory sick leave for which a team member may be eligible. This will be treated as a request for reasonable accommodation and will be evaluated accordingly. Team members should follow the [process in the Handbook for requesting this leave of absence](/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/time-off-types/#for-sick-absences-that-exceed-6-consecutive-days).

_Training and Career development_ - GitLab does not discriminate in providing training opportunities and any such opportunities would be available to all team members regardless of their protected characteristics. Every effort will be made to accommodate any special needs of team members by making reasonable adjustments to working arrangements. This applies to all aspects of employment, including training.

GitLab will endeavour to provide course materials meant for induction and training in accessible formats on request. Team members should endeavour to make a request for any reasonable accommodation, such as assistive aids, accessible training venue, accessible materials, interpreter etc at least one week prior to the scheduled date of commencement of induction/training.

### 1.8 Model Policy under HIV/AIDS Law

All team members are required to familiarize themselves with the Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome Policy for Establishments 2022 (Model Policy) notified under the HIV/AIDS Law. The Model Policy can be accessed [here](https://naco.gov.in/sites/default/files/HIV_and_AIDS_Policy_for_Establishments_2022_0.pdf).

### 1.9 Reporting and Escalation

GitLab does not tolerate discrimination and if a team member believes they have been discriminated against, have observed or learned of unlawful discrimination, or believes that any other violation of this EOP has occurred, they must report the suspected discrimination or violation right away and never retaliate against anyone who raises a good faith belief that unlawful discrimination has occurred. Team members should follow [the discrimination reporting steps set out in the Handbook](/handbook/people-group/anti-harassment/#discrimination). 

### 1.10 Disability Data and Maintenance of Records

To comply with the requirements under applicable law, if or when provided, GitLab will collect and maintain data regarding team members with disabilities in relation to their employment, facilities provided and other necessary information. Team members with disability and those who acquire disability during their employment can voluntarily disclose this in Workday. Sharing this data is strictly voluntary and will be confidential. Team Members are under no obligation to provide GitLab with a diagnosis and GitLab will not initiate action against any team member for not providing the information. 

All team members and candidates shall have the right to disclose their gender, gender identity, disability status, protected medical status, etc. or to keep such information private. Any team members desiring to change their name, gender or disability status or any information relating to their medical health can update the information in Workday and/or contact the Team Member Relations or People Compliance Teams for support with a deletion request. All information collected by GitLab from any person, including team members, job applicants, third-party workers and interns / trainees regarding their protected characteristics, or information provided in the Self-Identification form will be kept strictly confidential in accordance with the applicable protection measures and managed in accordance with the [employee privacy policy](/handbook/legal/privacy/employee-privacy-policy/).
