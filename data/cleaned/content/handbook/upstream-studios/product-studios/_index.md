---
title: "Product Studios"
description: "Product Studios are the organizational units within Product Design at GitLab, aligned to product areas."
---

Each studio is a named grouping of stage groups aligned to a product area, with a senior design leader responsible for the work across those groups.

## Studios

- Security
  - Scope: Security and Software Supply Chain Security
  - Design manager: Marissa Henri
- Core DevOps + Data
  - Scope: Create, Plan, Verify, Package, and Data
  - Design manager: Gina Thisse
- Artificial Intelligence
  - Scope: AI-powered features and the Duo product suite
  - Design manager: Alex Kozovski
- PLG/Monetization/Platform
  - Scope: Growth, Fulfillment, Govern, and platform-wide product areas
  - Design manager: Emily Sybrant
