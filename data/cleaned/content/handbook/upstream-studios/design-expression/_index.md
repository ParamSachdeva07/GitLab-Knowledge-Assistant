## What we do

- **Shape the design language** - We define the visual vocabulary, motion, and ambient experience that give GitLab its identity across product, marketing, and web.
- **Build and maintain the Design System** - The [Pajamas Design System](https://design.gitlab.com/) is our shared foundation: tokens, components, patterns, and guidance that enable teams to build cohesive, accessible, high-quality experiences at scale.
- **Own accessibility** - We make the GitLab product and Pajamas Design System more accessible for everyone, partnering with designers and engineers across the organization.

## Our disciplines

- **[Design System](/handbook/upstream-studios/design-expression/design-system/)** - The Pajamas Design System: shared tokens, components, patterns, and guidance for building the GitLab product experience.
- **[Accessibility](/handbook/upstream-studios/design-expression/accessibility/)** - Making the GitLab product and Pajamas Design System more accessible for our users.
