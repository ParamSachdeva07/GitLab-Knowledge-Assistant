This page is a collection of GitLab enablement resources for partners. Its main goal is to onboard partners' technical pre-sales teams into our [Channel Partner Program](/handbook/resellers/). Other sales professionals and services engineers will find plenty of useful information here too.

If you're a new partner, go through this page from top to bottom, following the numbered chapters in order. An [Ecosystem Sales Manager](/job-description-library/sales/partner-account-manager/) and an [Ecosystem Solutions Architect](/job-description-library/sales/solutions-architect/#ecosystem-solutions-architect) will support you along the way.

## 1. General Knowledge

Resources that every GitLab partner should be familiar with.

**Finding what you need**: To search some of the websites below faster, you can use [LLM system prompts](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/llm-system-prompts).

### 1.1 Public Websites

* [**Official website**](https://about.gitlab.com/)

  GitLab's public website. It contains a lot of useful, non-technical information.

* [**GitLab.com**](https://gitlab.com/)

  The Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) version of GitLab.

* [**Official documentation**](https://docs.gitlab.com/)

  The documentation for GitLab, the product. It's mostly technical, and it covers everything from step-by-step tutorials to complex architecture diagrams.

* [**Handbook**](/)

  The documentation for GitLab, the company. It describes how the company behind the product operates.

  The most important handbook pages:

  * [**GitLab Channel Partner Program**](/handbook/resellers/)

    Everything about how the Partner Program works.

  * [**Partner Technical Presales Enablement**](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/)

    A collection of technical pre- and post-sales enablement resources. This POWER page references it multiple times.

  * [**Product sections, stages, groups, and categories**](/handbook/product/categories/)

    Each DevSecOps stage covered by GitLab is represented by a team of engineers in the product organization, as described in the [DevOps stages section](/handbook/product/categories/#devops-stages). There you can learn who is responsible for what. You'll also find links to each team's handbook pages, which explain how the teams operate, and to the documentation for the features they cover.

  * [**Architecture Design Documents**](/handbook/engineering/architecture/design-documents/)

    Design documents for new features that GitLab is working on or planning to work on.

### 1.2 Partner Portal

The [**Partner Portal**](https://partners.gitlab.com) is a dedicated website for GitLab partners. Unlike the public websites above, which are open to everybody, it is only accessible to partners and GitLab team members.

* [**GitLab Partner Portal Guide**](https://partners.gitlab.com/prm/api/objects/v1/asset/hugkbmggdhtg/_view)

  User documentation for the Partner Portal itself.

* [**GitLab Partner Program Guide**](https://partners.gitlab.com/prm/api/objects/v1/asset/vtktpfdn20l5/_view)

  The internal version of the [GitLab Channel Program Guide](/handbook/resellers/channel-program-guide/) handbook page. Unlike the public handbook, it contains information about financial incentives.

* **Asset Library**

  This is where you can find training assets and other partner-only materials: PDF documents, slide decks, and links to websites. You can open the Asset Library from the menu on the left side of the Portal.

  Most of the content here can be shared publicly, but not all of it. When sharing is not allowed, the asset's description says so explicitly.

  **Best practices:**

  * When reviewing slide decks from the Portal, download the deck and open it in Google Slides, Microsoft PowerPoint, or similar software. Some slides contain valuable speaker notes that are not visible on the Portal.

  * The partner team works hard to keep everything up to date, but some information on the Portal will inevitably be outdated. As a rule of thumb, ignore assets that are older than six months.

### 1.3 GitLab University

GitLab's learning platform. It hosts the training courses, learning paths, and certifications referenced on this page. To open the partner version:

1. Go to the [Partner Portal](https://partners.gitlab.com/).
2. Click **GitLab University** in the left-hand navigation.

{{% alert title="Important" color="warning" %}}
Always enter GitLab University this way. It signs you in with your partner account and opens the partner-only version of the site, with exclusive content and discounts on certification exams. After you sign in once like this, the direct GitLab University links on this page also work with your partner account.

## 3. Selling GitLab

GitLab is a complex product in the highly competitive market for development tools. The resources here will help you sell it successfully.

* **GitLab Sales Professional Accreditation**

  To get started on your selling journey, open the partner version of [GitLab University](#13-gitlab-university), then start the [Professional Sales Accreditation for Partners course](https://university.gitlab.com/learn/course/professional-sales-accreditation-for-partners).

* **GitLab Flex Foundations**

  To better understand [GitLab's consumption-based pricing model](https://docs.gitlab.com/subscriptions/gitlab_flex/), open the partner version of [GitLab University](#13-gitlab-university), then start the [GitLab Flex Foundations course](https://university.gitlab.com/courses/gitlab-flex-foundations).

* [**GitLab Value Framework**](https://partners.gitlab.com/prm/api/objects/v1/asset/cryafg7tweuz/_view)

  This document supports you in your sales conversations and helps you position GitLab to your customers and prospects. It's the perfect next step to build on the knowledge you gained from the Sales Professional Accreditation above.

  This is the same document that GitLab's own sales teams use in their day-to-day work. The information comes from GitLab leaders and our sales, marketing, and product teams. It has been validated by our customers and focus groups, and it aligns our value-based messaging with current market trends (AI, Security & Compliance, Value Stream Management, and so on) and customer priorities.

  Because GitLab competes in many product categories, sales conversations can easily get derailed and lost in feature-by-feature comparisons. With this framework of topics and discovery questions, you can structure your conversations around value and business outcomes instead. To make your conversations more impactful, use our strategic product and marketing messages, and connect your customers' requirements to GitLab's solutions and unique differentiators.

  Because of its sensitive nature, this document can't be downloaded and is only available for viewing on the Partner Portal.

* [**GitLab Platform Overview Partner Portal page**](https://partners.gitlab.com/px/-/gitlab-platform-overview)

## 4. Demoing GitLab

These resources will give you a deeper technical understanding of GitLab, so that you can deliver an effective demo to customers.

### 4.1 Environments

Request two [**NFR (Not-for-Resale) licenses**](/handbook/resellers/channel-working-with-gitlab/#not-for-resale-nfr-program-and-policy): one for SaaS and one for self-managed.

* Use SaaS for demos, because it's always a stable environment.

* Use self-managed to learn how to install and operate a GitLab instance, because you'll have full admin access there. To set up a self-managed GitLab instance, see the [Implementing a GitLab instance section](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/partner-implementation-services/#implementing-a-gitlab-instance) of our Partner Implementation Services handbook page.

### 4.2 Content

Our [Demo Architect](/job-description-library/sales/demo-architect/) team keeps the content of this repository up to date. Usage instructions are in the [README file](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-learn-labs/sample-projects/tanuki-racing/-/blob/main/README.md?ref_type=heads) in the root of the repository.

  At the time of writing (2026-07-21), the **Duo Agent Platform** module is available in a separate repository, [**DAP Swag Shop**](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-learn-labs/sample-projects/dap-swag-shop), which has its own [workshop instructions](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-learn-labs/sample-projects/dap-swag-shop/-/blob/main/RunBooks/DAP-Workshop.md).

## 5. Building Services

Building a compelling services portfolio around GitLab for your customers is no small task. We'd like to help you with that too.

### 5.1 GitLab Professional Services Engineer (PSE) Certification

To build a standardized level of technical expertise in your organization, we advise each of your technical team members to get this certification.

Note that taking the PSE exams costs money, but the learning courses themselves are free. The certification is mandatory only if you want to earn [GitLab Professional Services Partner (PSP)](/handbook/resellers/channel-program-guide/#the-gitlab-professional-services-partner-psp-program) status, and even then only for a certain number of employees.

If you decide to get certified, open the partner version of [GitLab University](#13-gitlab-university) and navigate to the [PSE Learning Path](https://university.gitlab.com/learning-paths/gitlab-certified-professional-services-engineer-learning-path-c1b1). That page also contains the free learning courses.

**PSE FAQ**

* **How much time does it take to get PSE certified?**

  Here are some guidelines for people with different skill levels (one day = 8 hours):

  1. GitLab users who are deeply familiar with the product: 3–4 days.
  2. DevOps professionals who are familiar with other products on the market but have no GitLab experience yet: 10–12 days.
  3. IT professionals without DevOps knowledge or GitLab experience: 15–20 days.

* **Do I have to take the exams in order?**

  No, it's up to you. You just need to finish all six exams to earn PSE status.

### 5.2 GitLab Duo Agent Platform Associate Certification

At the time of writing (2026-08-14), Duo Agent Platform (DAP) is not part of the PSE curriculum. Even so, we strongly recommend that partners become proficient in this important part of our product. To do that, open the partner version of [GitLab University](#13-gitlab-university), then use these resources:

* [GitLab Duo Learning Path](https://university.gitlab.com/learning-paths/gitlab-duo-learning-path-v100): free, self-paced online training.
* [GitLab Duo Agent Platform Associate Certification](https://university.gitlab.com/courses/certified-duo-agent-platform-associate): optional, paid.

### 5.3 Further Education

To help you put the knowledge you gained during the certification process into practice, our team has collected all the necessary technical resources on the [Partner Technical Presales Enablement handbook page](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement). Its [Post-Sales Implementation and Adoption Journey section](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/#4--post-sales-implementation-and-adoption-journey) is dedicated entirely to partner services. The two most important subpages referenced there are:

1. [Partner Implementation Services](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/partner-implementation-services)
2. [Partner Migration Services](/handbook/resellers/partner-enablement/partner-migration-services)

Think of these as the next step after the two hands-on exams (implementation and migration) in the PSE certification process. Those exams require you to understand the basics of these two important activities and carry them out. The two pages above give you the tools and information to take on real-life, complex, large-scale customer engagements.

## 6. Staying Up to Date

As a partner, you need to get to know GitLab well. Staying up to date is just as important. Use the resources below to do exactly that.

* **Partner Flash Newsletter**

  You can access this newsletter through the [My News menu item of the Partner Portal](https://partners.gitlab.com/px/news-on-demand). Make sure you are subscribed to all the news that is relevant to you.

* [**Building Pipelines Webinar**](https://partners.gitlab.com/px/-/building-pipelines)

  This webinar series covers GitLab-related topics in depth to help you get better at selling GitLab and providing services around it. On this page, you can register for upcoming sessions and explore the video recordings and presentation materials of all previous episodes.

* [**Champions Program**](/handbook/resellers/partner-champions-program/)

  This program enlists, supports, rewards, and recognizes members of the GitLab Partner community who make outstanding contributions to GitLab and our community around the globe. Regular meetings provide these members with the latest information and technical previews, and give them a platform to exchange ideas.

* [**What's New Website**](https://about.gitlab.com/releases/whats-new/)

  This page lists what's new in each monthly GitLab release and which features are planned next. The "Upcoming" section (behind the "See what's coming" button) serves as GitLab's public roadmap, with links to our backlog across different product areas.
