---
title: GitLab Self Managed Internal Licenses for Team Members
description: How to get a self-managed GitLab license for team-member use via self-service
---

Self-managed licenses for GitLab team-member use are self-service. Provision your
own test subscription through the staging Customers Portal by following
[Unlock Premium/Ultimate features on self-managed / GDK for team members](/handbook/support/internal-support/#unlock-premiumultimate-features-on-self-managed--gdk-for-team-members).

Team-member license requests are no longer processed as issues, so the Support
Super Form should not be used for this.
