Identity Engineering owns the architecture, security controls, and provisioning automation behind identity and access management at GitLab.

[Corporate IT / End User Services](/handbook/eta/corporate-it/end-user-services/) handles requests and enablement — ad-hoc access requests, access builds for temporary service providers and external collaborators, account resets, and end user support.

## What We Own

1. Okta system management and Okta authentication
1. Automated access request flows through Lumos (Identity Governance)
1. Provisioning architecture and orchestration
1. [Onboarding](/handbook/security/corporate/services/onboarding) and [offboarding](/handbook/security/corporate/services/offboarding) automation and overall design
1. [Role change (career mobility) provisioning](/handbook/security/corporate/services/role-changes)
1. [Service Accounts](/handbook/security/corporate/services/service-accounts)
1. ABAC and RBAC, AuthN and AuthZ policies, role entitlements
1. Cloud IAM for [AWS](/handbook/security/corporate/systems/aws), [Azure](/handbook/security/corporate/systems/azure), and [Google Cloud](/handbook/security/corporate/systems/google/cloud)
1. [Sandbox Cloud](/handbook/company/infrastructure-standards/realms/sandbox/)

See [CorpSec Engineering](/handbook/security/corporate/engineering/) for the full system owner table.
