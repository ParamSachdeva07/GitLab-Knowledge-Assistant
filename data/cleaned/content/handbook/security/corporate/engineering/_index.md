title: CorpSec Engineering
---


The Engineering team members are organized functionally based on the category of tech stack applications that we manage.

CorpSec owns identity and access management (IAM) for AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Cloud resource administration is owned by [Infrastructure Security](/handbook/security/product-security/infrastructure-security/) and the [Infrastructure Platforms](/handbook/engineering/infrastructure-platforms/) (SRE) teams.
