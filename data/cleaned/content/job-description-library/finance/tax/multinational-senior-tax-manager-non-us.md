## Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US)

The Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) reports to the Senior Director, Multinational Tax.

## Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) Job Level

The Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) is outlined in the [Job Levels](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kcDb-A2uwchPtTNSJON65BdqS9P0KQmNz0fbNMZMt_M/edit?gid=819074618#gid=819074618) resource.

## Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) Responsibilities

- Manages transfer pricing contemporaneous documentation and country by country reporting with outside service providers
- Manages and reviews quarterly transfer pricing invoicing
- Assists with transfer pricing audits
- Administers intercompany agreements
- Manages local income tax compliance (EMEA and APAC) with our outside tax service providers
- Manages local income tax and indirect tax audits
- Maintains cumulative book/tax differences for our multinational entities for local and US GAAP purposes. Works with our Accounting team to understand GAAP to statutory book differences.
- Assists with VAT/GST compliance and audits
- Works with our stock-based comp and payroll teams regarding per-country stock based compensation issues
- Provides information, as needed, to international local entity directors
- Works with our finance and treasury teams to fund multinational operations
- Project manage special projects, including establishment of new legal entities, and other corporate initiatives, as needed.
- Engage and assist with M&A, including acquisition structuring and integration.

## Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) Requirements

- The Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) will have Big-4 or comparable multinational company experience. Specifically, the candidate will have demonstrable, progressive experience in:
- Transfer pricing and functional analysis
- Intermediate ASC 740 knowledge
- Permanent establishment issues
- Local corporate tax rules (EMEA and APAC) and compliance project management
- Understanding of local country withholding tax rules for digital services
- Understanding of US corporate international tax rules
- Indirect tax knowledge (VAT, GST, DST)
- The Multinational Senior Tax Manager (Non US) will have a BS/BA in Accounting or Finance. An MST, MBT, or LLM or similar international designation in taxation is preferred. A CPA or similar international license is preferred.
